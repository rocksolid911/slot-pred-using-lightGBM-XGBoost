Which slot should we book?

Consult times are right-skewed, and running late is worse than sitting idle. So the right slot is not the average visit; it's the quantile set by how much worse an overrun is than idle time.

Lab 1.1

The cost of every slot

Each minute a visit runs past its slot costs \(c_{over}\); each idle minute costs \(c_{idle}\). Edit the visits or the costs and watch the cheapest slot move. The flat bottom is always where the share of visits below it equals \(\frac{c_{over}}{c_{over}+c_{idle}}\).

Lab 1.2

The pinball loss

Divide the slot cost by \(c_{over}+c_{idle}\) and you get the loss a quantile model minimises:

\[ L_q(y,\hat y) = \begin{cases} q\,(y-\hat y) & y \ge \hat y \;\;\text{(ran over)}\\ (1-q)\,(\hat y-y) & y < \hat y \;\;\text{(idle)} \end{cases} \]

The loss decides what the model predicts
Train withThe model learns
\((y-\hat y)^2\)the mean
\(|y-\hat y|\) (pinball, q = 0.5)the median
