The score to beat

Before training anything clever, measure what simple rules already achieve. Every method here learns only from January–May and is scored on the 5,418 June visits. The q values (0.67, 0.75, 0.80) are the PO's business settings from A1; the PO picks one in A8.

Lab 5.1

Score = late × q + idle × (1 − q)

A few visits of the same kind, and one booked number for all of them. Move the booked number and watch each visit's late or idle minutes and the average score. The lowest point of the curve is always at the q-point of the visits: that's why each box books its q-point.
