One honest look at July–August

Every choice was made on June. Then the chosen model was refitted on January–June (with June's tree counts) and scored once on the locked test months. That single score is the answer to "how good is it?". The live demo is the OPD Slot Predictor.

Lab 8.1

Peeking makes the score look better than it is

Imagine several model versions that are equally good: each would truly score 1.40 on future visits. Any one test month adds luck, so its measured score wobbles around 1.40. If you try many versions on the test months and keep the one that looks best, you also keep its good luck. Move the slider and see how far the reported score drifts from the truth.
