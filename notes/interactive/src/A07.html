How a tree grows: leaf by leaf vs level by level

1,200 simulated visits described by two columns: prior reports (across) and patient age (up). Visits with many reports, and patients aged 65+, take longer. Each tree cuts this map into boxes and books each box's P80. LightGBM always splits the one box where a split lowers the score most. XGBoost splits every box on the current level, then goes one level deeper. Give both the same number of boxes and compare.
